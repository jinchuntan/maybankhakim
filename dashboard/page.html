How the signal works

The dashboard uses two fixed rules on the monthly close.

The trend rule uses the 10-month average. This is the mean of the last 10 monthly closes. When the close is above this average, the trend is up. When the close is below it, the trend is down. A buy signal is the month when the close crosses from below to above the average. A sell signal is the month when the close crosses from above to below the average.

The momentum rule uses the RSI (Relative Strength Index) over 14 months. It compares the size of the up months with the size of the down months. It goes from 0 to 100. Above 70 means the price rose fast. Below 30 means the price fell fast.

Buy / hold zone. The close is above the 10-month average and the RSI is 70 or lower.

Hold, do not add. The close is above the 10-month average and the RSI is above 70.

Wait, oversold. The close is below the 10-month average and the RSI is below 30.

Sell / avoid zone. The close is below the 10-month average and the RSI is 30 or higher.

Limits. The rules use past prices only. They do not use news, earnings or dividends. A crossing can switch back in the next month, so a signal can be wrong. The prices are not adjusted for dividends.
